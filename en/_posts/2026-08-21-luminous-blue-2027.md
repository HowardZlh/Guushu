## A colour of the year announced two years ahead

Pantone picks its colour in December and it covers the following twelve months. WGSN, the trend forecaster, and Coloro, the colour-system company, run on a different clock. Their Colour of the Year 2027 was fixed on 2 June 2025. It's called **Luminous Blue**, a bright blue with a lit-from-inside quality.

The two-year lead exists because the audience is people who make clothes. Mills need to dye yarn and brands need to plan ranges long before anything reaches a shop floor. In April this year WGSN already announced its 2028 colour, a warm earth tone named Radiant Earth.

### Why blue, and why this blue

WGSN's theme for 2027 is interconnectedness. Luminous Blue gets compared to two materials. One is lapis lazuli, the stone painters ground into ultramarine pigment for centuries. The other is cobalt, a key ingredient in the lithium-ion batteries behind today's energy transition. Old craft on one side, current infrastructure on the other.

Clare Smith, WGSN's Senior Colour Strategist, called it "both mysterious and eccentric" and said it would work from occasionwear to activewear. The announcement also stressed that the shade is gender-inclusive and not tied to a season.

### Did it actually turn up on the runway a year later

It did, and there's a number for it. For the spring 2027 womenswear shows in New York this September, WGSN ran every collection through its image-recognition tool. Blue rose 2.4 percentage points on the previous season, with Luminous Blue the standout shade. Retro blues rode along with it, described by WGSN as calm and a little nostalgic.

A forecast issued two years out, then caught by data on the runway. That's roughly the outcome a forecasting business hopes for.

### How this relates to Pantone

Two separate systems. Pantone speaks to designers and the general public and announces each December for the year ahead. WGSN and Coloro speak to the supply chain, about two years ahead. Pantone's 2027 colour won't be revealed until this December; we'll cover it then.

### Wearing a bright blue

- **Let one piece carry it.** A cobalt dress or a blue knit against white or black. The colour does the talking.
- **Blue on blue.** Bright blue layered with navy or denim wears better than blue against a contrast colour, and it's closer to what the retro blues on the runway looked like.
- **Start small.** A scarf, a bag or a pair of shoes if you're not sure.
