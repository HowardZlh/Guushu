## A season counted rather than described

Back in February we explained how fashion month is organised. This time the angle is different: instead of asking which show made the most noise, look at what got counted. On 15 September 2026 WGSN published its analytics for the spring 2027 womenswear shows in New York: 104 collections, 3,120 looks and 4,639 individual items, all run through its image-recognition tool, Fashion Vision.

The unit is the percentage point. A category up 1.7 points means roughly two more looks in every hundred wore it than last season. Small on paper. But when a hundred-odd brands making independent decisions all shift the same way, something is pulling.

### Two skirts the numbers picked out

**Column dresses** rose 1.7 points. A column dress runs almost straight from shoulder to ankle with no drama at the waist. New York designers gave it richer fabrics, stronger colour and small changes in cut, positioning it as a staple that goes from day to evening.

**Pencil skirts** rose 1.4 points. Full and A-line skirts are still the bigger growth drivers; the pencil came back by other means, through blocks of contrasting material and texture, seasonal prints and saturated colour. Old shape, new treatment.

![A row of dresses in strong colour on the runway](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1733322992706-1210ca79f4df?w=800)

### More blue, plus a few reds and a yellow

In the colour column, blue rose 2.4 points, the biggest of the figures WGSN published. WGSN's own Colour of the Year 2027, Luminous Blue, led it, with a group of retro blues alongside.

The top hashtag on WGSN's trend stream, The Feed, was **#BrightBasics**. The idea is simple. Take the plainest pieces in the wardrobe, a shirt, a straight skirt, a knit, and make them in Luminous Blue, Crimson, Tomato red or Carambola yellow. Familiar shapes in new colour are, in WGSN's reading, the easiest route into bold colour for most people.

### Using the numbers

- **Watch for repetition, not spectacle.** A category rising across a hundred collections is a better signal than one startling look at one show.
- **Old shape, new colour.** Column dresses, pencil skirts and shirts are already in most wardrobes. This season's answer for them is a colour change, not a cut change.
- **Wait for the other three cities.** London, Milan, Paris: not counted yet. New York skews commercial and wearable, so its numbers may not be fashion month's numbers.
