## Building a quiet luxury wardrobe: fewer pieces, better ones

A quiet luxury wardrobe runs on one decision: move the budget from quantity to quality. Quiet luxury itself means understated design, high-quality materials and timeless cuts, with no visible logos. For where the term came from and why it spiked in 2023, see [quiet luxury explained](/en/2025/02/05/quiet-luxury-explained/). This piece is only about the closet.

### 1985: Donna Karan's Seven Easy Pieces

The phrase capsule wardrobe was in American publications by the 1940s and was revived in the 1970s by Susie Faux, who owned a London boutique called Wardrobe. Donna Karan turned it into a show.

In 1985 she launched Seven Easy Pieces to fill what she called a void in the market for working women. Eight models walked out in nothing but bodysuits and black tights, then added clothes one at a time: wrap skirts, trousers, dresses. A handful of pieces that all work with each other. That is the whole idea.

### Rule one: fabric, judged by hand

Most of the quiet luxury feel comes from the fiber. Two of the most-cited houses show how far that goes.

Loro Piana was founded in Quarona, Italy, in 1924 as a merchant of cashmere, vicuña, linen, merino. It began working with vicuña in 1994, signed an agreement with Peru in 1997 to take fiber only from living animals, and in 2008 helped fund the country's first private vicuña reserve.

Brunello Cucinelli started his company in 1978 with the lira equivalent of about $550. The first product was cashmere sweaters dyed in bright colors. Fiber first, style second, in both cases.

### Rule two: a cut that ignores the season

The life of a garment is the life of its shape. Drop shoulders, straight legs and a lightly marked waist don't expire when one season's fad moves on. For how to judge a jacket's shoulder line and length, see [modern tailoring](/en/2025/04/24/modern-tailoring-styling/).

### Rule three: a narrow palette

The Wikipedia entry for quiet luxury captions a Brunello Cucinelli image with beige, white, sage. Add gray, navy, camel. That is the whole range. Few colors is what lets seven pieces make dozens of outfits.

### The pieces: Vogue's list from March 2023

On March 6, 2023, Vogue published a shopping list for the trend. The pieces on it that earn their keep:

- a cinched-waist blazer, or one strong coat
- a layering knit in cashmere or fine wool
- straight or wide-leg trousers
- a white T-shirt or tank, plus a button-up shirt
- one leather bag without a loud logo

The same article described the look as "a battered Hermès Kelly bag or a Max Mara investment coat thrown over an ancient pair of vintage jeans." Battered and ancient are the operative words.

### No logo, so what shows the quality

Details: even stitching, flat seams, drape that falls where it should, cloth that feels right in the hand. None of that reads in a product photo. So a quiet luxury wardrobe tends to be bought slowly, one piece at a time, in person.
