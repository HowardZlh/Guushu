Looking Ahead to 2027

🔵 Colour of the Year 2027: Luminous Blue

WGSN, the trend forecaster, and Coloro, the colour-system company, named Luminous Blue their Colour of the Year 2027 back in June 2025. The shade is compared to lapis lazuli on one side and cobalt on the other: centuries of pigment history and the metal inside lithium-ion batteries. At the spring 2027 shows in New York this September, blue rose 2.4 percentage points on the previous season. This is a separate system from Pantone, whose 2027 colour won't be announced until December 2026.

Source: WGSN, "Colour of the Year 2027: Luminous Blue" (2 Jun 2025) | Key shades: Bright blue, cobalt, retro blues

📊 New York Spring 2027, Counted

WGSN ran image recognition over 104 collections and 3,120 looks at New York Fashion Week spring 2027. Column dresses rose 1.7 points and pencil skirts 1.4 points, both old shapes in new fabric and colour. The top hashtag was #BrightBasics: shirts and straight skirts in bright blue, crimson, tomato red or carambola yellow. London, Milan and Paris have not been counted yet.

Source: WGSN, Kim Cupido, "S/S 27 New York Women's Fashion Week Trends: Analytics" (15 Sep 2026) | Keys: Column dress, pencil skirt, bright basics

🚲 Copenhagen Spring 2027: Long Shorts, Basket Weave, Rococo

A month ahead of the big four, Copenhagen Fashion Week's 20th edition showed spring 2027 in August. Polka dots and lingerie dressing carried over from 2026; panniers, crinolines and corsets brought historical romance; long shorts with a crisp shirt were the everyday answer. Basket-woven textiles, swim references in daywear and plush textures were the city's own additions.

Source: Vogue, Laird Borrelli-Persson, "The Trends That Made a Splash at the Spring 2027 Shows in Copenhagen" (12 Aug 2026) | Keys: Long shorts, basket weave, historical romance

🎸 Four Directions Called Before the Shows

On 25 August WGSN named four directions for the spring 2027 womenswear runways: New Glam (leather, animal print, Saint Laurent-style rock glamour), Free-Style Expression (pastels, clashing patterns, self-styled maximalism), Put-together City Dressing (relaxed prep with polka dots and plaids layered) and Romantic Vintage (sheer slips and lace with raw edges and asymmetric trim). Worth checking against the runways once the big four wrap.

Source: WGSN, Kim Cupido, "What do trend forecasters expect to see on the S/S 27 Womenswear catwalks?" (25 Aug 2026) | Keys: New Glam, city dressing, romantic vintage

🖤 Resort 2027: The Little Black Dress at 100

Chanel's resort show opened with Matthieu Blazy's little black dress. In 1926 Vogue called Chanel's dress fashion's "Ford"; 2026 marks the centenary. Drop-waist dresses from the 1920s, silk and chiffon rosettes and 1930s-style ruched gowns returned in the same season, alongside carpenter pants with hammer loops and crisp button-down shirts.

Source: Vogue, Laird Borrelli-Persson, "Function, With a Side of Frisson—The Resort 2027 Trends That Matter" (18 Jun 2026) | Keys: LBD, drop waist, carpenter pants

2026 Fashion Trends

🕊️ Color of the Year: Cloud Dancer

The Pantone Color Institute named Cloud Dancer (Pantone 11-4201) its 2026 Color of the Year — a soft, almost neutral off-white. After 2021's Ultimate Gray, the honor again lands on the quietest end of the spectrum: not an attention-grabbing color but a blank space, and therefore easier to wear and to combine. It echoes a wider contemporary taste for the quiet and the clean.

Source: Pantone Color of the Year (announced Dec 2025) | Key shades: Off-white, oatmeal, ivory, cream

🤍 Quiet Luxury & the Off-White Wardrobe

Quiet luxury, building since the early 2020s, converges in 2026 with the off-white color of the year into a single thread: fewer logos, low saturation, letting texture and silhouette speak. Wearing an all-off-white look well isn't about adding color but layering different fabrics (knit, poplin, wool), then finishing with a relaxed silhouette and one leather or metal accessory.

Season: From early spring | Keys: Same color/different texture, silhouette first, neighboring pales

🌾 The Return of Natural Fabrics

As the weather warms, linen becomes almost summer's default language. Woven from the bast fibers of the flax plant, it conducts heat quickly, is highly absorbent, and dries faster than cotton, making it born for the heat; its signature natural creases are now seen as an honest beauty rather than a flaw. Belgium and Italy remain important sources of high-quality linen.

Season: Spring/Summer | Fabrics: Linen, cotton, natural fibers

🧵 The Value of Craft and Handwork

From haute couture, shown each January and July, to the espadrille with its nearly seven centuries of history, a major thread in 2026 is a return to the value of hands and time. A couture piece can take hundreds of hours, settling into draping, hand embroidery, and beadwork — craft is becoming another yardstick for measuring the worth of a garment.

Focus: Couture ateliers, traditional shoemaking | Keys: Embroidery, draping, slow craft

♻️ Sustainability as the Norm

Eco-conscious fashion has long shifted from a selling point to a standard. From natural materials like crochet and linen to recycled fabrics and low-waste production, sustainability is no longer just for premium buyers but a choice anyone can join — buying less and wearing longer is itself a form of sustainability.

Focus: All year | Materials: Natural fibers, recycled fabrics, natural dyes

Seasonal Trend Report

Early Spring · Off-White Leads

Echoing Cloud Dancer, layer neighboring pales — oatmeal, ivory, cream — and let silhouette replace contrast.

High Summer · Linen & Espadrilles

Breathable linen paired with jute-soled espadrilles — a relaxed, put-together answer for both holiday and everyday.

Autumn/Winter · Textured Layers

Checked wool, knits, and outerwear layered together — warmth without bulk through understated fabrics and clean silhouettes.
